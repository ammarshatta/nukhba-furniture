# Custom Furniture in Cairo: What to Know Before You Order

Cairo has a substantial furniture manufacturing industry. From the workshops of Damietta to the showrooms of the Fifth Settlement, custom furniture is widely available across every price point. But the quality range is enormous — and the difference between an excellent and a disappointing custom piece often comes down to questions asked before the order is placed.

## Why Custom Furniture Makes Sense in Egypt

Egyptian apartments have specific proportions — ceiling heights, room widths, and layout configurations — that often don't align with standard European furniture dimensions. A 3-meter sofa that looks proportionate in a European showroom can overwhelm an Egyptian apartment living room.

Custom furniture also allows for fabric and finish choices that align with your existing interior — matching existing floor colors, complementing existing furniture, or achieving a specific color palette that catalog products don't offer.

## Questions to Ask Any Custom Furniture Maker

Before placing a custom order anywhere in Cairo, ask these questions:

1. **What wood species is used for the frame, and what is the moisture content?** (Answer should be hardwood, kiln-dried to 8–10%)
2. **Can I see examples of your previous work in person?** (References should be verifiable)
3. **What is included in the warranty, and what is excluded?** (Frame warranty of at least 2 years is standard in the premium market)
4. **What deposit is required, and what are the payment milestones?** (50% at order, 50% at delivery is reasonable; requests for 100% upfront are a risk signal)
5. **Who manufactures the pieces — your own workshop or a subcontractor?** (Brand responsibility differs significantly)

## Red Flags to Watch For

**Too-short lead times**: A sofa genuinely made from solid wood with quality foam cushioning takes a minimum of 3–4 weeks. A 2-week turnaround almost certainly involves pre-made components.

**Inability to explain materials**: If a salesperson cannot specifically name the wood species, foam density, or fabric composition, the likelihood is that lower-quality materials are being used.

**Pressure to decide immediately**: Quality furniture purchases should not involve sales pressure. A reputable manufacturer has a backlog and will hold your specification without requiring an immediate commitment.
