## Venezia Modular Bookcase

The **Venezia** is Nukhba's answer to one of modern living's most persistent challenges: how to store and display a growing book collection beautifully. The system is built from solid walnut in a modular format, allowing each owner to configure it to their specific wall and needs.

### Modular by Design

Each standard Venezia unit is 120cm wide × 220cm tall × 35cm deep. Units connect seamlessly using concealed alignment hardware, creating the appearance of a built-in library. The system accommodates both standard paperbacks and oversized art books, with adjustable shelf heights in 32mm increments.
