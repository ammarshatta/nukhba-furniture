## Riviera Executive Desk

The **Riviera** desk was designed for the Egyptian professional who demands both function and aesthetics from their home office. The frame is fabricated from 4mm powder-coated steel, CNC-cut for precision, then assembled with M8 bolts into a rigid structure that eliminates desk wobble — a common failure in lighter alternatives.

### The Walnut Desktop

The 40mm-thick solid walnut top is the desk's defining feature. Each top is selected for a consistent, straight grain pattern and finished with a matte hardwax oil that resists spills and scratches while remaining repairable.
