## Empire Bedroom Suite

The **Empire** collection embodies the Nukhba philosophy: furniture that ages gracefully and gains character over time. This complete bedroom suite is constructed from premium solid oak and bookmatched walnut veneer, with brushed brass hardware that adds warmth without ostentation.

### Complete Suite Contents

The Empire suite is designed as a cohesive ensemble. Every piece shares the same material palette and hardware finish, creating a bedroom environment that feels curated rather than assembled.

- **King Bed Frame** — Solid oak slats, upholstered headboard in performance linen
- **Two Bedside Tables** — Two-drawer configuration with soft-close mechanisms
- **6-Door Wardrobe** — Full-height mirror on center panels, interior lighting
- **Dressing Table & Stool** — Integrated LED lighting, genuine leather stool

### Built for Egypt's Climate

Wood furniture in Egypt faces specific challenges: significant temperature variation and humidity fluctuations. The Empire suite uses kiln-dried timber with a moisture content of 8–10%, preventing warping and joint separation over time.
