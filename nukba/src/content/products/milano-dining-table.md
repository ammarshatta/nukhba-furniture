## Milano Solid Oak Dining Table

The **Milano** dining table is a testament to what solid wood can achieve when shaped by skilled craftspeople. The table top is constructed from single-plank European oak boards, selected for grain consistency and minimal knots, then hand-finished with a natural hardwax oil that deepens the color while protecting the surface.

### The Case for Solid Wood Dining Tables

In Egypt, the dining table is more than furniture — it is the center of family life. A dining table purchased from Nukhba is built to last generations. Unlike veneered or MDF alternatives, the Milano can be refinished multiple times, restoring it to its original appearance even after years of use.
