## The Palazzo Velvet Sofa Set

The **Palazzo** is Shatta's signature living room statement. Inspired by contemporary Italian design studios, this 3+2 sofa set combines the tactile luxury of hand-selected Italian velvet with the warmth of solid walnut joinery.

### Why Egyptian Homeowners Choose the Palazzo

Egyptian homes have a unique requirement: furniture that feels generous and welcoming for family gatherings, yet sophisticated enough for formal occasions. The Palazzo delivers both. Its deep cushioning — filled with high-density foam and topped with natural feather — provides exceptional comfort for long evenings, while its clean lines and premium finish make it the visual centerpiece of any room.

### Materials & Construction

The frame is constructed from kiln-dried hardwood, ensuring dimensional stability in Egypt's climate. Joints are reinforced with steel brackets for longevity. The solid walnut legs are hand-finished with a matte oil treatment that highlights the natural grain.

### Customization

Available in three standard colors: Forest Green, Charcoal, and Warm Beige. Custom colors and dimensions available on request through our design consultation service.
